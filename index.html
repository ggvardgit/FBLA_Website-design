Chronology

Timeline of American History

A concise path through the major turning points that shaped the United States—from early societies to the modern era.

1491

Native societies flourish before sustained European contact.

1607

Jamestown founded—first permanent English settlement in North America.

1776

Declaration of Independence articulates revolutionary ideals.

1787

Constitution drafted; federal system established.

1803

Louisiana Purchase doubles the nation's territory.

1861–1865

Civil War tests union and ends slavery.

1865–1877

Reconstruction redefines citizenship and federal power.

1890s

Gilded Age industrialization transforms economy and labor.

1917

United States enters World War I.

1929

Great Depression begins; New Deal follows.

1941

Entry into World War II reshapes global role.

1947–1991

Cold War defines foreign and domestic policy.

1950s–1960s

Civil Rights Movement expands American democracy.

2001–Present

Modern America: security, technology, and polarization.
